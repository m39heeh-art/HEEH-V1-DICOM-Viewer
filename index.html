About HEEH-V1

HEEH-V1™ is an open-source medical imaging research and education platform. It brings image review, calibrated measurements, quantitative imaging, radiomics-oriented outputs, experimental AI tools, and provenance-aware export into a single reproducible workflow.

DICOM & NIfTI Review Review medical images and volumetric datasets in a lightweight web interface.
Calibrated Measurements Perform measurements with calibration support for research-oriented imaging workflows.
Quantitative Imaging Explore quantitative imaging metrics and structured analysis outputs.
Radiomics-Oriented Analysis Generate radiomics-oriented outputs within a reproducible research workflow.
Experimental AI Explore experimental artificial intelligence tools for medical imaging research.
Provenance-Aware Export Export structured outputs designed to support transparency and reproducibility.
Research and education use: HEEH-V1 is intended for research, education, prototyping, and reproducible imaging workflows. It is not intended as a diagnostic medical device or a replacement for clinical imaging software.
